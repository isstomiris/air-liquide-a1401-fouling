# Slide: How the cleaning options compare

| | Air blowing | Water wash | Chemical / soap wash | Coating + sound horns (ours) | DECAMEX dry powder (ours) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hot-day outlet (no cleaning: 75 °C; plant's shutdown clean: 57 °C*) | No data | No data | No data | 67 °C | 63 °C |
| Plant must stop? | No | No (often done at shutdown) | No (often done at shutdown) | Once, to apply the coating | No |
| Uses water? | No | Yes | Yes (rinse) | No | No |
| Risk to metal (rust, scale, bent or etched fins) | Medium | Medium–high | High | Low | Low |
| Waste to handle | Dust blown around | Dirty water | Chemical-laden water | Dust collected | Dry dust |
| Proven on cottonwood fluff? | No | Yes, one plant | No | Not yet | Not yet |
| Already used in industry? | Yes | Yes | Yes | Parts only | Yes |

\* The plant cleaned A-1401 during its 2026 shutdown. Air Liquide has not said which method it used.

**Bottom line:** the plant's shutdown clean cools best. Our two methods are dry and run during the season without stopping the plant, so the fluff never piles up between shutdowns. Next step: a pilot test on real cottonwood.
