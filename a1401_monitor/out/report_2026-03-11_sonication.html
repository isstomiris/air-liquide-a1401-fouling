How this is computed

1) Each hour, the physics model of A-1401 (fin efficiency, Briggs & Young air side, fan and system curves, condensing gas side) finds the blocked fraction that explains the measured outlet temperature, given inlet temperature, gas flow, fan speed and outside air. This removes the effect of weather and load. 2) Gradient-boosted trees forecast that number 7/14/30 days ahead; the band is calibrated (conformal) on simulated plants the model never saw. 3) Acoustic/ultrasonic cleaning removes loose, fresh deposits best, so it is run early and often.

Hours used: 18533 · rejected: missing: 560, low_load: 604, flaring: 266, unsteady: 595, inlet_below_outlet: 471 (rules: missing data, load < 30 %, flaring, unsteady, inlet not hotter than outlet). Limits: the blockage is an equivalent figure (it also includes louvres, fan wear and recirculation); the forecaster was trained on simulated fouling cycles because only one week of real data was available.
